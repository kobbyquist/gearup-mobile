See it in action

The owner experience

Everything a car owner needs, in one clean app. Scroll through each screen below.

Login 1 / 7
Sign In

Welcome back

A clean, simple login screen. Enter your email and password and you're in. Forgot your password? Reset it with one tap.

  • Email and password login
  • Forgot password flow built in
  • Secure session management
Getting Started

Up and running in seconds

Create your account as a Car Owner or Mechanic. Registration takes under a minute — name, email, phone, and a secure password.

  • Choose your role: Car Owner or Mechanic
  • Real-time password validation
  • OTP verification for account security
Home

Your dashboard at a glance

The home screen shows your vehicles, a one-tap SOS button, quick actions to post a job or find a mechanic, and your most recent job updates.

  • SOS emergency button front and centre
  • All vehicles listed with service history
  • Recent jobs with live status
Find a Mechanic

See every mechanic near you, live on the map

Switch between Map and List views. Tap any pin for distance, address, and a live route — then request with one tap.

  • Live map with pins and routing
  • List view with distance and availability
  • Star favourites for quick rebooking
  • Browse spare parts in the same screen
SOS Emergency

Stranded? Get help in three taps

Hit SOS, pick your vehicle, and GearUp finds the nearest mechanic instantly. Review, confirm, and your GPS is shared automatically.

  • Nearest mechanic found within seconds
  • Vehicle and GPS pre-filled
  • Mechanic notified immediately
Jobs & Chat

Track every job, chat in real time

My Jobs keeps all requests organised by status, grouped by week. Chat lets you share photos, voice notes, and job cards with payment confirmation in the thread.

  • Filter by Pending, Accepted, In Progress, Completed
  • Photos and voice notes in-chat
  • Payment confirmation in thread
Wallet & Profile

Your balance, your vehicles, your account

The Wallet shows your live balance and full transaction history. Profile manages your vehicles with make, year, plate, and last service date.

  • Live balance with transaction history
  • Top up via Paystack securely
  • Manage multiple vehicles
Sign In

One login, two roles

Mechanics use the same secure login. After signing in, the app detects your role and loads the mechanic dashboard automatically.

  • Email and password login
  • Role-aware session — mechanic view loads instantly
  • Secure session management
Home

Your mechanic dashboard

See your availability status, a summary of today's jobs, and quick access to browse open requests near you.

  • Toggle availability on/off instantly
  • Today's job summary at a glance
  • Quick links to jobs and earnings
Available Jobs

Browse and accept jobs near you

See all open job requests sorted by distance. Tap any card to review the full details — vehicle, issue, and location — before accepting.

  • Sorted by distance from your location
  • Full job details before committing
  • One-tap accept
Job Details

Everything you need before you go

See the owner's vehicle, the problem description, their precise location on the map, and the proposed price — all before you accept.

  • Vehicle make, model and year
  • Owner location on live map
  • Set your own quote before accepting
Active Job

Navigate and update in one screen

Once accepted, navigate directly to the owner. Update job status as you go — Accepted, In Progress, Completed — so the owner always knows what's happening.

  • Turn-by-turn navigation to owner
  • One-tap status updates
  • Owner notified at every step
Chat

Agree on the job before you start

Chat directly with the car owner. Clarify the problem, share your diagnosis, send a quote, and get approval — all within the thread.

  • Real-time messaging with the owner
  • Send and receive photos
  • Price proposal and approval in-chat
Parts Market

List parts, reach more customers

Sell spare parts directly through GearUp. List items with photos, set your price, and reach car owners actively looking for trusted suppliers.

  • List parts with photos and pricing
  • Visible to all nearby owners
  • Manage your inventory in one place
Earnings

Track every cedi you earn

Your earnings screen shows daily, weekly, and monthly totals. Every completed job is logged with the amount and date so nothing slips through.

  • Daily, weekly, and monthly breakdowns
  • Full job-by-job transaction log
  • Withdraw to your bank anytime
Wallet

Get paid, withdraw fast

Payment lands in your GearUp wallet the moment a job is confirmed. Check your balance, view transaction history, and withdraw to mobile money or bank.

  • Instant payment on job completion
  • Full transaction history
  • Fast withdrawal to mobile money
Profile

Your professional profile

Your public profile shows your specializations, rating, completed jobs, and customer reviews. A strong profile earns more trust — and more work.

  • Specializations and service areas
  • Star rating and review count
  • Completed jobs history
Availability

Work on your schedule

Toggle availability on or off at any time. Set your working hours so you only receive job requests when you're actually ready to work.

  • One-tap online/offline toggle
  • Set custom working hours
  • No jobs pushed when you're offline
Notifications

Never miss a job request

Push notifications alert you the instant a new job comes in near you. Tap to review the full details and accept before another mechanic does.

  • Instant push for new job requests
  • Payment and chat alerts
  • Manageable notification preferences
Reviews

Build your reputation one job at a time

Every completed job earns a rating and review from the car owner. Your overall score is displayed publicly to help new customers choose you with confidence.

  • Star ratings from every completed job
  • Written reviews visible on your profile
  • Higher ratings attract more bookings
